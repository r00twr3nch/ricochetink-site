Privacy policyGizlilik politikası

Last updated 26 November 2026Son güncelleme 26 Kasım 2026

Ricochet Ink is a physics puzzle game developed by [COMPANY NAME] ("we"). The game is intended for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains what data the game and the services inside it handle.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content. Your level progress, stars and unlocked ball designs are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The iPhone and Android versions show ads served by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad when you ask for a hint or an extra shot, and a short interstitial ad after every few completed levels. Ads are never shown while you are aiming.

Ad requests are marked as child-directed. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating G), and they are not personalized based on your interests. On Android the game does not request access to the advertising ID.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect limited information such as your IP address, device type and operating system, and how you interact with an ad. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The game contains no analytics and no other third-party services. Apart from loading ads, it does not connect to the internet, and it can be played offline.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is designed to comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and applicable local law, including the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK.
